Privacy policy

Effective October 12, 2026. Applies to the Fay app for iPhone, its widgets and its share extension.

Fay keeps your lines on your iPhone. There is no account and no server, and we can't see what you save. Fay has no ads, no analytics and no tracking, and it doesn't send your lines anywhere.

What Fay stores

The lines you save and what you add to them: the sentence, the passage it came from, its short lock-screen version, the author and work, themes, and when you saved it. If a line came from another app, Fay also keeps the page title and a short name for the site it links to (“Kindle”, “Safari”), not the link itself. Fay also remembers which principle is on your lock screen and how often each one has been shown.

All of this is stored on your iPhone, in a space that only Fay, its widgets and its share extension can open. It is included in your iPhone's own backups, like other app data.

Screenshots and photos

Reading screenshots is off until you turn it on. When it is on, each time you open Fay it looks at the screenshots you have taken since the last time, reads their text on your iPhone using Apple's built-in text recognition, and keeps that text in Fay's inbox until you keep a line or clear it. Screenshots with no text are skipped. Fay stores the recognised text and a reference to the screenshot so you can open it again; it doesn't copy the image.

iOS has no read-only access to Photos, so Fay asks for access to your library, but it only ever reads screenshots. It never changes, deletes or uploads your photos. If your photos are in iCloud Photos, iOS may download a screenshot from your iCloud so it can be read; that happens between your iPhone and Apple.

You can turn this off in Fay's Settings (“Read new screenshots”), or remove Fay's access in the iPhone Settings app under Privacy & Security › Photos. A screenshot you pick by hand when adding a line is read the same way, on your iPhone.

Suggestions

When you save a line, Fay suggests a short version for the lock screen and a theme or two. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence it uses Apple's on-device language model; otherwise it uses simple rules. Either way it happens on your iPhone, and nothing is sent to us or to anyone else.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no sign-in.
  • No ads, no analytics, no crash reporting services, no third-party code that collects data.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • We don't sell or share your data, because we never receive it.

Exporting and deleting

You can export every line as a text file from Settings › Export all lines. You can delete any line from its page in the app. Deleting Fay from your iPhone deletes everything it stored.

Children

Fay is suitable for all ages and collects no information from anyone, children included.
