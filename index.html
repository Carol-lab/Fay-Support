Questions

How do I put a principle on my lock screen?

Touch and hold your lock screen, tap Customize, then Lock Screen, then Add Widgets, and choose Fay. It shows one principle a day and moves to the next one tomorrow. For the home screen, touch and hold an empty spot, tap Edit, then Add Widget.

How do I save a line from another app?

Select the words, tap Share, then Fay. If Fay isn't in the row of apps, scroll to the end, tap More and turn it on. You can also tap + in Fay to paste or type a line.

What is the difference between a spark and a principle?

A spark is a line you met somewhere: a book, a page, a conversation. A principle is a line you tell yourself and don't change much. Sparks become principles when you distill them, or when you choose "Make this a principle".

What does Fay do with my screenshots?

Only if you turn it on, Fay looks at screenshots you have taken since you last opened it, reads their text on your iPhone and suggests the sentence worth keeping. Screenshots without text are skipped. Nothing is uploaded, and Fay never changes or deletes your photos.

To stop, turn off "Read new screenshots" in Fay's Settings, or remove Fay's access to Photos in the iPhone Settings app.

Does Fay use AI?

On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Fay uses Apple's on-device model to suggest a short lock-screen version of a line and a theme or two. It runs on your iPhone and nothing is sent anywhere. On other iPhones, Fay makes the same suggestions with simple rules.

Where are my lines kept? Can I back them up?

On your iPhone, inside Fay. They are part of your iPhone's own backup, and you can export every line as a text file from Settings, Export all lines. Deleting the app deletes them, so export first if you want to keep a copy.
